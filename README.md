# Kanyunyuzi's Kitchen

A Ugandan homegrown waffle and bakery venture focused on freshly made, affordable-premium waffles and convenient doorstep delivery.

## Project purpose
This repository is the source of truth for the business concept, product and menu development, operating procedures, financial planning, brand assets, and the customer-facing website/PWA.

## Current concept
- Business: Kanyunyuzi's Kitchen
- Category: Bakery / freshly prepared waffles
- Market: Uganda; Pearl Marina is an initial target market
- Preparation: Waffles made fresh per individual order
- Positioning: Affordable-premium
- Sales direction: Direct customer orders with doorstep delivery
- Business model: Sole-proprietor operation

## Principles
1. Be transparent about ingredients and customer-selected preferences.
2. Keep ordering simple and mobile-friendly.
3. Price products using measured costs, not guesswork.
4. Distinguish confirmed decisions from assumptions and proposals.
5. Protect customer information and never commit secrets or credentials.
6. Validate kitchen operations before scaling the digital experience.

## Status
Repository foundation and Phase 2 planning documents are in place. The standard recipe, exact pricing, final menu, delivery terms, and implementation stack still require validation or confirmation.
