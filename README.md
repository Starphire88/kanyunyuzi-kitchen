# Kanyunyuzi's Kitchen

A Ugandan homegrown waffle and bakery venture focused on freshly made, affordable waffles and convenient doorstep delivery.

## Project purpose
This repository is the source of truth for the business concept, product and menu development, operating procedures, financial planning, brand assets, and the future customer-facing website/PWA.

## Current concept
- Business: Kanyunyuzi's Kitchen
- Category: Bakery / freshly prepared waffles
- Market: Uganda
- Sales direction: Direct customer orders with doorstep delivery
- Initial product focus: Waffles, flavour choices, and waffle bundles
- Business model: Sole-proprietor operation

## Repository map

## Principles
1. Be transparent about ingredients and customer-selected preferences.
2. Keep ordering simple and mobile-friendly.
3. Price products using measured costs, not guesswork.
4. Distinguish confirmed decisions from assumptions and items still to validate.
5. Protect customer information and never commit secrets or credentials.
