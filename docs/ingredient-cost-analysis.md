## 3. Fat-cost interpretation

- 110 g Lato butter costs UGX 5,060 at the supplied quote.
- 110 g Blue Band Original costs UGX 1,210 if the pack is 500 g, or about UGX 1,344 if the pack is 450 g.
- The Original spread therefore reduces the fat-line cost by approximately **73.4%–76.1%** versus the quoted butter. The 76.1% figure applies at a 500 g pack size.
- 110 g from the low-fat 250 g tub costs UGX 1,760, about **65.2% less** than the quoted butter on a simple weight-price comparison.
- The 90 ml olive-oil trial costs UGX 8,100 for fat, about 60.1% more than the 110 g butter line on this recipe's quoted purchase prices.

A lower ingredient purchase cost does not prove an equivalent waffle result. Blue Band Original and low-fat spread are not automatically interchangeable gram-for-gram with butter in baking: formulation, water content, salt, melting behaviour, flavour, browning, and texture may differ. Test separate versions, record the exact product and pack label, and do not silently change the formula. The low-fat tub should be treated as a separate experiment, not the default substitution.

## 4. Cinnamon purchasing comparison

Based on the supplied quotes:
- Branded 100 g jar: UGX 15,000, or UGX 150/g.
- Loose 1 kg quote: UGX 15,000, or UGX 15/g.
- Arithmetic result: the loose quote is 90% cheaper per gram and supplies 10 times the quantity for the same stated outlay.

This is a price comparison only. Before adopting loose cinnamon for food production, verify that it is food-grade, clean, properly stored, correctly labelled, and consistent in aroma and quality. Confirm the actual net weight and supplier price at purchase. Cinnamon is not included in the current base recipe cost because no recipe dosage has been specified.

## 5. Waffle maker and electricity

Supplied machine specification:
- Belgian square waffle maker.
- Capacity: 4 waffles per cooking cycle (as supplied).
- Rated power: 1,100 W (1.1 kW).
- Voltage: 220–240 V.
- Frequency: 50/60 Hz.

At continuous rated draw, the theoretical energy use is 1.1 kWh per hour. Actual consumption depends on preheating, thermostat cycling, cooking duration, and usage conditions; rated wattage alone does not give the cost per batch.

**Electricity cost estimate** = measured kWh used × applicable tariff (UGX/kWh).

Measure or record warm-up time, cook time per cycle, number of cycles per batch, and energy use if a plug-in energy meter is available. Four-waffle cycle capacity is not the same as batch yield: the batter portion and number of cycles required are still unknown.

## 7. Current decision

Do not set public bundle prices from these partial subtotals. Run controlled butter, Blue Band Original, and olive-oil trials using identical non-fat ingredients and recorded portion weights. The Blue Band Original option is the lowest-cost fat line on the supplied quotes, but the final choice must also pass taste, texture, repeatability, and customer acceptance tests.
