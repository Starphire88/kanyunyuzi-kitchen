# Menu and Flavours

## Hero product
Freshly prepared waffles.

## Working base-recipe ingredient list
This is an ingredient disclosure draft, not a final standardised recipe:
- Flour
- Sugar
- Salt
- Baking powder
- Eggs
- Milk
- Butter or olive oil
- Flavouring / essence of choice

Exact quantities, method, yield, portion size, and final ingredient statement must be established through recipe testing.

## Flavour direction
Options discussed include vanilla, strawberry, and blueberry. These are proposed options, not a confirmed always-available menu. Availability and suitability should be validated, and additional ingredients or toppings must be listed accurately.

## Waffle bundles
Bundle names may combine Luganda and English. Before launch:
- Confirm wording and spelling with fluent speakers.
- Make contents and quantities obvious.
- State the price in UGX.
- Clarify flavour selection and mixing options.
- Ensure names do not obscure what customers receive.
