# Brand Guidelines

## Brand
**Name:** Kanyunyuzi's Kitchen

The name Kanyunyuzi means “little star” in Luganda, Runyoro and other Ugandan languages, according to the supplied brand concept.

## Positioning
An approachable, affordable, homegrown Ugandan waffle kitchen offering freshly prepared treats and convenient doorstep delivery.

## Brand personality
- Warm and welcoming.
- Friendly and locally rooted.
- Dependable and straightforward.
- Cheerful without feeling childish.
- Clear and honest about products, ingredients, and prices.

## Visual direction
The initial direction is an approachable bakery identity suitable for packaging, social media, and a mobile ordering experience. The logo concept may integrate a small star symbol into the typography, reflecting the meaning of Kanyunyuzi.

The final logo, colour palette, typography, and packaging system are not yet recorded as approved brand assets. Do not invent official brand specifications in production materials.

## Voice
Use concise, warm, customer-friendly language. Keep prices, bundle contents, ingredients, and ordering terms unambiguous. Luganda and English may be combined in bundle naming, with language reviewed before publication.
