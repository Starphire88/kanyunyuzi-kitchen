**Unit ingredient cost** = purchase price ÷ usable pack quantity.

**Ingredient cost per batch** = sum of (quantity used × unit ingredient cost).

## 5. Pre-order and scheduled fulfilment model

The current model is pre-order only, with waffles prepared each morning before workplace delivery and delivery/collection scheduled.

Track per service day:
- Orders received before cut-off.
- Confirmed orders.
- Waffles required by bundle size.
- Production batches and planned yield.
- Actual saleable yield.
- Scheduled collection / delivery slots.
- Delivery cost by route or zone.
- Late cancellations and no-shows.
- Actual revenue, variable cost, and contribution.

Production plan should be based on confirmed orders plus a separately measured allowance for rejects, not speculative overproduction.

## 6. Break-even framework

**Contribution per order** = average order revenue − average variable order cost.

**Break-even orders** = fixed costs for the period ÷ contribution per order.

Only calculate break-even when contribution is positive and fixed costs have been identified. State the period (e.g. per week or per month).

## 7. Data required before setting prices

- Approved recipe and actual saleable yield.
- Current supplier prices and usable pack sizes.
- Packaging type and cost for each bundle.
- Actual labour time for prep, cooking, packing, and order administration.
- Waffle maker wattage and electricity tariff or measured energy use.
- Delivery fee / route costs and any amount charged to customers.
- Payment method fees.
- Expected order mix by bundle size.
- Target contribution and any fixed overheads.

## 8. Pricing guardrails

- Never call revenue profit.
- Include the cost of labour, packaging, energy, and rejects.
- Separate product margin from delivery economics.
- Review costs when supplier prices change.
- Show all assumptions and dates beside calculations.
- Do not set public prices from competitor prices alone; verify that each price covers Kanyunyuzi's own cost structure.
