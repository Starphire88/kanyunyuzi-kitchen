# Customer Website / PWA Product Brief

## Purpose
Create a lightweight, mobile-first customer experience for discovering Kanyunyuzi's Kitchen waffles, selecting flavours and bundles, entering order preferences, and contacting the business to place or confirm an order.

The first release should prioritise a clear menu and a low-friction order enquiry. Do not assume online payment, automated delivery dispatch, customer accounts, or live inventory unless separately approved.

## Customer journey
1. Discover the kitchen and waffle offering.
2. Browse available products and bundles.
3. Choose a product, quantity, and flavour where applicable.
4. Enter contact and delivery details.
5. Voluntarily provide allergy, dietary, or other preparation notes.
6. Review the enquiry and submit it through the agreed contact channel.
7. Receive confirmation from the business.

## Product information
Each menu item should support a name, description, image, price in UGX, serving size or quantity, flavour choices, bundle contents, and availability status.

## Customer-provided information
Do not impose preselected allergy or dietary requirements. Provide optional, customer-editable fields:
- Allergies or ingredients the customer needs to avoid.
- Dietary preferences or requirements.
- Additional preparation notes.

Do not promise allergen-free products or absence of cross-contact unless ingredients, equipment, storage, and preparation processes reliably support that claim.

## Working ingredient disclosure
The supplied base ingredient list is flour, sugar, salt, baking powder, eggs, milk, butter or olive oil, and flavouring/essence of choice. The final public ingredient statement must match the standardised recipe actually used. Disclose flavour-specific ingredients and toppings.

## Language and tone
Use clear, friendly language suited to customers in Uganda. Waffle bundle naming may combine Luganda and English. Confirm spelling, meaning, and customer comprehension before publishing Luganda product names.

## UX requirements
- Mobile-first responsive layout.
- Clear menu and prices.
- Simple flavour and bundle selection.
- Accessible form labels and validation.
- Visible contact and ordering instructions.
- Fast-loading imagery.
- Clear distinction between an enquiry and a confirmed order.

## Out of scope until approved
Online payment integration, customer accounts, loyalty programme, automated rider assignment, delivery tracking, and complex inventory management.

## Initial release acceptance criteria
- Customers can view the menu and current prices.
- Customers can select items, quantities, and available flavours.
- Allergy, dietary, and preparation-note fields are optional and customer-entered.
- Enquiries include contact and delivery information.
- Customers can review details before submitting.
- The business receives enquiries through the agreed channel.
- The interface states that an order is not confirmed until the business confirms it.
