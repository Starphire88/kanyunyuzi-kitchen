# Customer Ordering MVP — Backlog

Priorities:
- **P0:** Required for a safe, usable first pilot.
- **P1:** Valuable improvement after the core flow works.
- **Later:** Defer until validated need.

## P0 — Menu and discovery
- [ ] Display business name, short description, service information, and contact route.
- [ ] Show only confirmed products and flavours.
- [ ] Show accurate prices in UGX.
- [ ] Show product images and serving descriptions.
- [ ] Disclose actual ingredients and relevant flavour-specific additions.
- [ ] Explain bundle contents and quantities.

## P0 — Selection and enquiry
- [ ] Select a product.
- [ ] Select quantity.
- [ ] Select an available flavour or bundle configuration.
- [ ] Prevent invalid combinations and unavailable options.
- [ ] Collect customer name and contact details.
- [ ] Collect delivery area / address details.
- [ ] Provide optional customer-entered allergy / ingredient-avoidance notes.
- [ ] Provide optional dietary preference and preparation notes.
- [ ] Show an editable order summary.
- [ ] Generate a clear order enquiry for the agreed contact channel.
- [ ] State that the enquiry is pending until the kitchen confirms it.

## P0 — Operational clarity
- [ ] Show current ordering hours or cut-off guidance once confirmed.
- [ ] Explain how delivery fees and final totals are confirmed.
- [ ] Provide a clear contact route if WhatsApp handoff fails.
- [ ] Avoid promising stock, delivery times, or allergy accommodations that have not been verified.

## P1 — Customer experience
- [ ] Add a frequently asked questions section based on real customer questions.
- [ ] Add customer feedback link after fulfilment.
- [ ] Improve product imagery and bundle comparison.
- [ ] Add basic event measurement without collecting sensitive customer notes.

## Later — Consider only after pilot evidence
- [ ] Persistent order database and admin dashboard.
- [ ] Online payments.
- [ ] Customer accounts and order history.
- [ ] Automated delivery allocation and tracking.
- [ ] Loyalty programme.
- [ ] Inventory and supplier management.

## Definition of done for P0
- Customer can complete the flow on a mobile device.
- Selected items, quantities, flavours, and bundle contents are represented correctly in the summary.
- Price estimate is clear and does not misrepresent delivery charges or confirmation.
- Optional notes are genuinely optional and customer-entered.
- Ingredient information is accurate for the actual product.
- Business can receive the enquiry and manually confirm or decline it.
- A test order has been run end-to-end by the business before public promotion.
