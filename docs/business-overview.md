# Business Overview

## Concept
Kanyunyuzi's Kitchen is a small Ugandan homegrown bakery venture centred initially on freshly prepared waffles. The concept is to make waffles accessible through affordable offerings, flavour choices, bundles, and convenient doorstep delivery.

The name **Kanyunyuzi** means “little star” in Luganda, Runyoro and other Ugandan languages, as described in the business concept.

## Initial business model
- Sole-proprietor business.
- Waffles as the initial hero product.
- Direct-to-customer ordering.
- Afternoon and evening operating window is the current intended schedule.
- Doorstep delivery is part of the intended customer proposition.

## Customer proposition
- Freshly made waffles.
- Clear flavour and bundle options.
- Ingredient transparency.
- An uncomplicated way to place an order and communicate preferences.
- Affordable positioning supported by properly calculated costs.

## Priorities
1. Standardise and test a repeatable waffle recipe.
2. Validate customer preferences and serving sizes.
3. Establish reliable procurement and production routines.
4. Calculate contribution margin and sustainable selling prices.
5. Test delivery packaging and product quality after transit.
6. Launch a simple digital ordering experience.

## Guardrails
Do not publish unverified claims about nutrition, allergens, shelf life, or delivery times. Ingredient and allergen information must reflect the actual recipe and preparation process.

## Known equipment and budget context
- A waffle maker is already owned.
- Its wattage and exact model have not yet been recorded here.
- Initial procurement budget discussed: UGX 100,000–200,000.
- A final standard recipe and validated unit economics have not yet been established.
