## Recipe decisions still open

- Standard recipe quantities, method, yield, and portion size.
- Butter versus oil: compare both through testing and costing.
- Final flavour list and availability.
- Toppings, bundle contents, and any additional ingredients.

## Product and technical decisions still open

- Final website builder / framework.
- Hosting and domain.
- Whether WhatsApp is the confirmed initial order channel.
- Whether an order database or admin dashboard is required at launch.
- Whether PWA installation and offline behaviour are needed at launch.
- Final customer data retention and privacy approach.

## Proposals, not decisions

- Use WhatsApp as the first order handoff to minimise operational complexity.
- Launch with a small menu and expand only after customer and production validation.
- Defer payments, accounts, loyalty, inventory, and delivery automation until there is a demonstrated need.
- Keep the first implementation backend-light if a manual order confirmation workflow is sufficient.

## Change control

When a proposal becomes a confirmed decision, record who confirmed it, the date, the rationale, and any consequences for the product or operations.
