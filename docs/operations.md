# Operations

## Intended operating window
The model is pre-order only. Waffles are prepared fresh each morning before workplace delivery, with delivery or collection scheduled in advance. Exact order cut-off times, operating days, and fulfilment slots remain to be confirmed.

## Production setup
- A waffle maker is already owned.
- Record its make, model, wattage, plate size, and operating instructions.
- Standardise batter quantities and preparation method.
- Measure cooking time and output under repeatable conditions.
- Record batch yield and rejected units.
- Establish appropriate cleaning, food handling, and storage procedures.

## Procurement
An initial procurement budget of UGX 100,000–200,000 has been discussed. This is a planning range, not a confirmed spend or complete operating budget.

Track supplier, pack size, purchase price, date, and usable quantity for every ingredient and packaging item.

## Order fulfilment
1. Receive pre-order enquiry before the published cut-off.
2. Check product and flavour availability.
3. Confirm contact and delivery details.
4. Confirm price, delivery fee, and estimated fulfilment time.
5. Accept and confirm the order.
6. Prepare and quality-check the waffles.
7. Package to preserve product quality.
8. Arrange handover or delivery.
9. Record payment and completion.
10. Collect feedback where appropriate.

## Open decisions
- Operating days and order cut-off times.
- Waffle maker specifications and production capacity.
- Final standard recipe, yield, and batch time.
- Packaging and delivery method.
- Delivery zones, fees, and fulfilment promise.
- Customer ordering channel.
- Food safety and allergen-handling procedures.
- Payment methods and reconciliation.
- Exact pre-order cut-off and scheduled delivery / collection slots.

Do not advertise delivery coverage, preparation times, or food-safety claims until operationally verified.
