## Unit economics framework
For each batch, record ingredient and quantity used, purchase unit and pack size, price, cost per gram/ml/unit, total batch ingredient cost, saleable yield, packaging, labour, electricity, fees, and wastage.

**Ingredient cost per saleable waffle** = total batch ingredient cost ÷ saleable waffles per batch.

**Variable cost per order** = product cost + packaging + order-specific costs + any business-funded delivery cost.

**Contribution per order** = selling price − variable cost per order.

**Contribution margin** = contribution per order ÷ selling price × 100.

Keep delivery fees distinct from product revenue and costs. Revenue is not profit.

## Pricing principles
- Price each item and bundle from actual contents and costs.
- Include packaging and realistic wastage.
- Decide whether delivery is charged separately or subsidised.
- Test customer willingness to pay without pricing below sustainable cost.
- Revisit prices when supplier costs change.
